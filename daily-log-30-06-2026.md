## One Thing That Was New
The connection between **normalisation and detection rules**. I understood normalisation as a formatting step, but hadn't fully thought through what breaks if you skip it — detection rules rely on specific field names, and if those field names differ across log sources, the rule silently misses events. Normalisation is what makes one rule work universally across Windows, Linux, and firewalls.
